# Google Play listing: Herbal Holland Rewards

App name (30): Herbal Holland Rewards

Short description (80): Your Herbal Holland member card, points and vouchers. For adults 18+.

Full description:

Herbal Holland Rewards is the member app for Herbal Holland stores in South Africa. It is for adults 18 and over.

Show your digital member card at the till to earn points on every purchase and spend them on your next visit. Your balance, tier and points history are always up to date, in store and in the app.

What you can do in the app:
- Show your member card and QR code at the till
- See your points balance, tier and full points history
- Keep your vouchers and member offers in one place
- See your in-store purchases and tax invoices
- Refer friends with your own link and earn store credit when they shop
- Find your nearest Herbal Holland store, with opening hours and directions
- Read Learn, our guides to products and the law in South Africa
- Get help from our team

Joining is free with your mobile number. We only use your details to run your membership, and you can download your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.

The app does not sell products. Purchases happen in store.
