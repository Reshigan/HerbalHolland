# Google Business Profiles

One profile per retail store (not the distribution centre), owned by admin@herbalholland.com. Each profile's website is
that store's page on the shop, so "Website" clicks land on the right address, hours and directions. The same details are in
[google-business-profiles.csv](google-business-profiles.csv) for Google's bulk upload, which only pays off from 10 stores;
with four, add them one by one.

## Before you start

- Sign in at business.google.com as admin@herbalholland.com, with two-step verification on.
- Each store needs its own phone number and opening hours before verification (Google calls or checks against them).
  Missing today: Bluff and Pietermaritzburg hours and every store's phone.
- Verification is per store, usually a short video: the street sign or centre entrance, the shopfront with the signage,
  and inside (till, stock, a staff member) in one take. Have the signage up first.

## Name and category

- Name: **Herbal Holland**, exactly as on the signage. Google suspends profiles that add keywords or places to the name;
  a descriptor such as "Herbal Holland Bluff" is only allowed if that is what the sign says.
- Primary category: type "CBD" in the category box and take "CBD store" if Google offers it; otherwise "Health and beauty
  shop". Additional: "Clothing store" (apparel), "Hydroponics equipment supplier" (grow).
- Never choose a medical category (pharmacy, dispensary, medical clinic).

## After verification

- Turn on messaging only if a person will answer within a day; otherwise leave it off.
- Ask members at the till for a review: print the profile's "Ask for reviews" link as a QR at the counter.
- Answer every review within two days, in the brand's voice, never quoting what someone bought.
- Post the launch campaign's store posts as Google "Updates" (no prices or promotions on cannabis or CBD).
