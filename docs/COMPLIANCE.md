# Compliance by design

How the platform encodes South African rules. Background, dates and sources:
[research/COMPLIANCE-RESEARCH.md](research/COMPLIANCE-RESEARCH.md). **This is not legal advice** — have counsel sign off
the settings below before launch and whenever the law moves.

## Legal position (as researched on 27 Sep 2026)

- Private adult use, possession and cultivation are decriminalised (*Prince*, 2018). The Cannabis for Private Purposes
  Act (2024) is **not yet in force**; draft regulations were published in February 2026; a Cannabis Bill for commercial
  trade is expected from mid-2027. On 18 Sep 2026 the Justice Minister confirmed the regulations still need Parliament.
- Commercial THC trade is therefore lawful only within **SAHPRA licensing**. Herbal Holland states it is
  SAHPRA-registered; the platform's default mode matches that: **MEDICAL**.
- Lawfully sellable today: Schedule 0 CBD within limits (complementary medicines, and foods with ≤ 0.0075% naturally
  occurring CBD), hemp products (≤ 2% THC hemp; non-consumables), accessories, apparel, grow equipment (seeds: unclear —
  review). Adjacent wellness lines without cannabis (collagen, plain protein) are sold as foodstuffs.

## The compliance engine

Pure rules in `src/lib/compliance.ts`, evaluated server-side for every POS sale and online order
(`src/server/compliance.ts → evaluateBasket`). The rules are **data** (`HQ → Compliance`, stored in `Setting`), so they
change without a deploy.

| Setting | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `thcMode` | `MEDICAL` | Licensed products need an active patient authorisation with monthly headroom and an authorised dispenser. `ADULT_USE` switches to verified adults with a daily limit. |
| `licensedOnly` | `THC` | Classes sellable only at stores whose `licences` include them (per-store, with `licenceRef` printed on invoices). |
| `minAge` / `ageRestricted` | 18 / THC, CBD, CBD_FOOD, CBD_TOPICAL, HEMP, ACCESSORY, GROW | ID check required (member verified, ID sighted, or SA ID number checked); under-18s blocked even if an ID was sighted. Supplements (no cannabis) aren't age-restricted by law — every store is adults-only anyway. |
| `channels` | THC: POS + click & collect only | Class × channel matrix (POS, COLLECT, SAME_DAY, COURIER). |
| `noIncentives` | CBD | Classes that get no promotions, member or volume prices, markdowns, points, My Deals, welcome gifts or staff commission (Medicines Act s18A). CBD supplements are Schedule 0 complementary medicines, so they're listed until counsel clears them; licensed classes are always excluded in MEDICAL mode. HQ → Compliance → "No promotions, points or commission". |
| `dailyLimitGrams` | 30 | ADULT_USE daily cap per customer (posted sales + open orders). |
| `tobaccoBillMode` | off | When Bill B33-2022 commences: related products (pipes, papers, vapes) become in-store only and earn no points. |

## Trade (wholesale)

The trade channel sells to businesses — pharmacies, independent retailers, cafés, wellness practices, wholesalers —
never to the franchise stores and never licensed medicines. `evaluateTradeBasket()` (`src/lib/compliance.ts`) is the
only basket rule it uses; `src/lib/trade.ts` holds the pricing, case, credit and ledger rules, and
`src/lib/trade-licences.ts` the licences a buyer must hold.

| Obligation | Where it's enforced |
|---|---|
| Medicines Act s22A — who may be supplied | Schedule 0 CBD and the excluded products (CBD food and drink, skin care, hemp, supplements, accessories, apparel, grow) may be sold to any business. THC is never traded: `tradeAllowed()` says no whatever the account or the seller holds, `tradeQuote` prices it at nothing and the basket rule names the line. Each account carries the classes HQ approved it for (`TradeAccount.classes`, never THC); the selling store must hold a licence for any class in `licensedOnly` |
| Medicines Act s18A — incentives | A consumer rule: trade quantity breaks and TRADE list prices are prices, not bonuses, and apply to every class. Promotions reach a trade basket only when HQ adds the WHOLESALE channel to them, and even then medicines (`incentiveExcluded`) get none. No points, member prices, My Deals or gift vouchers on trade |
| VAT Act s20 — full tax invoices | Every WHOLESALE sale is a full tax invoice (a credit note for a refund): the seller's legal name, address and VAT number, and the buyer's legal name, address and VAT number from the account (`Sale.buyer`), numbered `TI-…` for orders or by the till for counter sales; unit prices ex VAT, VAT worked out per line (15%, rounded half up to the cent), the total incl. VAT. A non-vendor franchisee's till issues an "Invoice" without VAT, as SARS requires |
| CPA — juristic customers | The Act largely doesn't apply to juristic persons above its asset/turnover threshold, but the trade terms (`trade.termsVersion`, accepted on application and stored on the account) are still plain and fair: prices ex VAT with VAT shown, the minimum order and case packs before ordering, delivery and handling fees from settings, EFT orders held three days, invoices due after the agreed days, credit limits applied the same way every time (`creditDecision()`), short shipments invoiced only for what went, a refund by hand named on a cancelled paid order |
| Licences the buyer holds (business policy; counsel to confirm) | `requiredLicences(kind, classes)` decides what an account must hold for its kind of business and the ranges it buys: **pharmacy** — the licence to own the pharmacy (Pharmacy Act 53 of 1974 s22, Department of Health) and its SAPC recording; **health practice** — its practice number (BHF PCNS, with HPCSA/AHPCSA registration; lapses yearly); **café** — a business licence (Businesses Act 71 of 1991, meals and perishable food; the municipality) and a certificate of acceptability for the premises (R638 of 2018); **retailer or online shop** — a company gives its registration certificate (Companies Act 71 of 2008, CIPC; the account's registration number), a sole proprietor (no registration number) its SARS tax registration (Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011 s22: the income tax reference number and the notice of registration); **distributor, other** — must be companies: the CIPC certificate; **distributor buying CBD supplements** — a SAHPRA licence to wholesale or distribute (Medicines Act s22C(1)(b)); **anyone buying CBD food and drink** — the certificate of acceptability. Mandatory at `/trade/apply` (number, expiry where it lapses, and the document: PDF/JPG/PNG ≤ 5 MB, its type told by its bytes, nothing stored until every document passes). HQ compares each document with the issuer's register and verifies or rejects it (the reason is emailed), audited (`trade.licence.submit` / `.verify` / `.reject`). `licenceStanding()` is "verified and in date"; approval, a change of kind, ranges or registration number (company or sole proprietor) on an approved account, portal and HQ orders, shipping or handing over an order, and till trade sales all refuse while a required licence is missing, waiting, rejected or lapsed (a renewal waiting for HQ doesn't interrupt a licence still in date). The daily job (`tradeLicenceExpiry`) reminds 30 days before a licence lapses and puts an approved account whose licence has lapsed ON_HOLD (reason "licence"); verifying the renewal lifts it. Documents are served only through the app (`/trade/licences/[id]/file`) to HQ and the account's own buyers, every view audited (`trade.licence.file_view`) |
| 18+ | Applicants attest that everyone who orders for the business is 18 or older (`adultAttestedAt`); the collector's ID is checked at a click & collect handover (`idChecked`), and the sale records `ID_SIGHTED` |
| POPIA | Buyers are business contacts: a name, work email and optional phone; sign-in codes are emailed and logged without the code; the application notice to the trade team carries the business, not the contact details; closing an account ends every buyer's session. Licence documents can carry personal information (a sole practitioner's registration, a sole proprietor's tax number): they are private objects in storage, never linked publicly, shown only to HQ and the account's buyers; the application form's IP is kept only as a keyed hash to limit repeat applications (five an hour) |

## Gift vouchers (CPA s63) and reorder reminders

**Gift vouchers are money, not an incentive.** A prepaid voucher is consideration the member already paid; it carries
no bonus value on purchase, earns nothing on top, and is never discounted. So it pays for anything in the range —
licensed medicines included, as a matter of law (Medicines Act s18A forbids inducements, and a voucher offers none).
It is redeemed online at checkout or at the till as a tender like any other, medicines included: the till checks the
voucher on the server and redeems it as the sale posts (never on a sale queued offline).
**Validity: three years** from the date of issue — Consumer Protection Act s63(2) — enforced by `expiresAt` (set when
the voucher is issued, not when it is bought), refused at checkout after that, and marked EXPIRED by the daily job.
Unspent balances stay on the voucher until then (s63(3)); the liability report is the sum of active balances.
**No cash-out.** A voucher can't be exchanged for cash, and a refund never turns one into cash either: what a voucher
paid on a sale goes back onto that voucher first (`allocateRefund`), at the till and for online returns alike, and only
what was paid in money is refunded in money. VAT: none at issue; accounted for on the sale it pays for (VAT Act
s10(18) treatment of vouchers), which is what the posted sale — at the till or at handover — records.

**Reorder reminders are transactional, not marketing.** A reminder exists only because the member set it, on the
channel they chose, for one product, with an interval they picked, and they can stop it in one tap. It is a service
message about that request, so it is sent regardless of the marketing consents (POPIA s69 governs unsolicited
direct marketing; CPA Reg 4's contact hours apply to direct marketing) — but the content is confined to the reminder
and the one-tap link, with no offers, no promotions and no other products, so it never becomes marketing by the back
door. Every send is logged (`MessageLog`, kind TRANSACTIONAL), and setting or stopping a reminder is audited
(`reminder.set`, `reminder.cancel`). No charge is ever taken: there is no stored card until Peach Payments approves
tokenisation, and a reminder never places an order — the member does, from their bag.

**Wishlists and saved addresses** hold personal information (what a member wants; where they live) and are shown
only to that member; the address goes to the fulfilling store on the order itself, as before. Both are deleted with
the member's account.
