# Press pitches

Earned media is the channel no platform can switch off. This pack is for the owner to send from
press@herbalholland.com: one personal email per outlet, never a mass mail. Each points to the press room at
herbalholland.com/press (fact sheet, logos, notes on how to write about us) and the release it goes with.

## Before the first send

1. **Approve the release.** "Herbal Holland opens in KwaZulu-Natal" is drafted in
   `sites/herbalholland.com/content.ts` (PRESS.releases) with `date: null`, so it isn't public. Read it, then give it
   the day it goes out (YYYY-MM-DD) and redeploy herbalholland.com: it appears at /press/kwazulu-natal-stores-open.
2. **A founder quote.** Two sentences in your own words: why Herbal Holland, why KwaZulu-Natal. Journalists need a
   named person; we never invent one. It goes into the release's body and every pitch below.
3. **Pictures.** At least the Bluff and Pietermaritzburg shopfronts with signage, and one inside shot, shot to
   docs/PHOTOGRAPHY.md (no consumption, no leaves, no smoke, everyone over 25). Send them on request; don't attach.
4. **Store hours** on the release once you have them (journalists will ask "when can readers visit?").

## The angles (pick one per outlet)

- **Local opening:** two KZN stores open, two more on 1 December; locally owned franchises; free membership.
- **Dutch heritage, African soil:** a brand named for Amsterdam's coffee-shop history, rooted in KZN's own landraces
  (Durban Poison left from the hills behind Durban in the 1970s). Good for features and radio.
- **Doing it by the book:** members only, ID at the till, adults 18+, CBD within Schedule 0 and licensed medicines
  only through a pharmacist. A clear contrast to the grey market.
- **The law, plainly (op-ed):** what changed with the Cannabis for Private Purposes Act and what didn't, what Schedule
  0 CBD is, what a retailer may and may not sell. Offered as a byline piece, not an advert.

Never in a pitch: prices, offers, health claims, anything about THC products for sale, or "dispensary".
