About this guide, and who does what

How to use this manual, the eight surfaces of the platform, and the roles — from a dispenser at the till to the HQ administrator — with what each can see and do.
In this chapter

    How to use this guide

    This is the reference manual for the Herbal Holland platform: one Next.js application that runs the shop, the Rewards app, every till, every store's back office and the HQ console, plus the separate corporate website at herbalholland.com. It describes what the software does — as built — not what was planned.

    The guide was written from the code itself (every screen, action and rule), the project documentation, and screenshots taken from the automated test journeys against the seeded demo network. Where the documentation and the code differ, the code wins; a footnote says so where it matters to you.

    • Read chapter 2 first if you are new: it explains how everyone gets in, and how to install the three apps.
    • Members and patients live in chapter 3. Store teams should read it too — it is what your members see.
    • Dispensers and cashiers need chapter 4 (the till) and the compliance rules in chapter 10.
    • Store managers and franchisees need chapters 4, 5, 10 and 11.
    • HQ staff and area managers need chapters 6 to 11.
    • The appendices hold the demo logins, the catalogue import columns, a glossary and the open decisions.

    Procedures are written as numbered steps in the second person ("Tap Charge"). Rules the software enforces are set out in orange callouts; things that can go wrong, in amber ones. Money is in rand; retail prices include VAT; wholesale prices and costs exclude it. Times are South African (SAST). We say members, never customers, and everything is for adults 18+.

    A word on the demo

    Screenshots come from the demo network (24 stores, about 18,000 members, 60 days of trading) with DEMO_MODE=true: names, numbers and prices are made up, payments are simulated, and one-click logins exist that a live system must never have. Chapter 11 says how production differs.

      Staff sign-in: email, password and the forced first change

      1. Open /login, enter your work email and password, and press Sign in.
      2. You land where your role lives: HQ roles on the HQ console, a store manager on their store, an owner on All my stores (or their single store), a budtender on the till.
      3. If you were given a temporary password (every new HQ person, and managers created by an owner), the platform sends you to /account/password before anything else. Enter the current password, then a new one of at least 10 characters that isn't the one you were given, twice. Changing it signs you out of every other device.
      Lock-outs and sessions
      • Eight wrong attempts on one email lock that email for 15 minutes.
      • A back-office session lasts 7 days; sign out on shared computers.
      • A deactivated account can't sign in at all, and a person can never deactivate themselves; there must always be at least one active HQ administrator.
      • "Your account doesn't have access to that page" means the page is outside your scope — not a wrong password.

      Passwords are never stored in clear; sessions are random tokens kept only as hashes in secure, HTTP-only cookies. Every login (and every demo login) is written to the audit log.

      The till: pairing a device, then a PIN

      A till is any phone, tablet or computer with Chrome. It is paired to a store once; after that, staff unlock it with a personal PIN, and it keeps selling cash even when the connection drops.

      Pairing (once per device)

      1. On the device, open /pos/pair. If you are on Android, accept Install the till app first (it is optional, but the installed app opens full-screen and keeps its shell offline).
      2. Sign in with a store manager, owner, area manager or HQ email and password (or, if that person is already signed in to the back office on this browser, they are recognised).
      3. Choose the store (only trading stores you may pair are listed) and give the till a name of up to 40 characters, such as "Counter 2" or "Pop-up tablet". The name appears on receipts and Z-reports.
      4. Press Pair this till. The device gets a letter (A, B, C…) that prefixes its receipt numbers — PTA01-B00042 means store PTA01, till B, sale 42 — and a pairing that lasts a year.

      Unlocking with a PIN (every shift)

      1. Tap your name on the lock screen, then enter your 4-digit PIN.
      2. The till stays unlocked for you for 12 hours, but locks after 5 minutes idle; tap Lock when you walk away. Tap your name and re-enter the PIN to carry on — your basket is kept.
      3. Offline, the till still unlocks for anyone who signed in on it during the last 12 hours (it keeps a scrambled check of the PIN, never the PIN itself).
      4. When a sale needs a different person — an authorised dispenser for a licensed medicine, or a manager — the till asks that person to unlock instead; the basket is kept.

      PINs are set by the store manager or owner in the back office (Team). They must be 4 digits with no repeated or running digits, and are handed over in person. Only a store manager can unpair a till (Shift › This till), and only with no shift open; unpairing wipes the till's cached data (sales waiting to sync are kept).

      Members: a code by SMS, no passwords

      Members never have a password. Anywhere the shop needs to know who you are — the Rewards card, checkout, a drop, an event — it asks for your South African mobile number and sends a 6-digit code by SMS.

      1. Enter your mobile number (any format; it is stored as +27…). Press Send my code.
      2. Type the code from the SMS (phones can autofill it). It expires after 10 minutes; you get 5 tries per code and at most 5 codes in 10 minutes.
      3. If the number is already a member, you are signed in for 30 days on that device. If not, one short step completes your membership (chapter 3).

      If the SMS can't be sent, the shop says so and asks you to try again in a minute — it never pretends a code went out. Sign out from Rewards › Settings.

      Demo mode

      With DEMO_MODE=true (local, staging and the demo — never production):

      • /login shows one-click logins for four roles; the password for every seeded person is holland. Appendix A lists them.
      • /api/auth/demo?email=…&next=/hq signs in a seeded user directly (used by screenshots and smoke tests).
      • The pair screen offers one-click pairing to any store, and the demo PINs are 1111 (a manager who is also a dispenser), 2222 (a dispenser), 3333 and 4444.
      • Member sign-in shows the SMS code on screen.
      • Payments are simulated (chapter 9) and no messages leave the system — every SMS and email is only logged.
      Warning

      Production must run with DEMO_MODE=false, a strong SESSION_SECRET and a CRON_SECRET. The launch checklist in chapter 11 repeats this because it matters.

      The online shop and Herbal Holland Rewards

      One national shop for the whole network, and one free membership that works in every store and online. Members pay less instantly, earn points worth R1 each, and see exactly what is in every batch.

        Joining: the 18+ check, membership terms and consents

        We sell to members only, and only to adults. Joining is free, takes about a minute, and needs no password and no plastic: the card lives on the member's phone.

        1. Confirm your mobile number with the code sent by SMS (chapter 2). A number that is already a member is simply signed in.
        2. First name (required) and an email for tax invoices (optional — invoices and receipts are emailed after every purchase if you give one; marketing email only if you also tick it).
        3. Confirm you are 18 or older with your SA ID number — checked instantly (format, checksum, date of birth, 18+) and stored only as a secure fingerprint plus the last four digits — or choose Passport and enter your date of birth; passport holders show their document at a store on their first visit, and until then can't order age-restricted items online.
        4. Choose your home store (where you usually collect; a store that is opening soon can be chosen too).
        5. News, drops & offers: tick the channels you're happy to hear on — WhatsApp, SMS, email, app notifications. Nothing is ticked for you. The exact wording you saw is stored with your answer.
        6. Optionally say what you're into (CBD & wellness, accessories, merch & apparel, grow your own, events & drops) so deals are relevant. Licensed medicines are never an interest.
        7. Read the key membership terms and accept the membership terms and privacy notice. Joining is refused without acceptance; the version you accepted (Version 1 · 27 September 2026) and the time are recorded on your membership.

        You then have a member number (HH0100001 style), a referral code, 25 welcome points, and Seed prices from your first basket. Members can also be signed up at the till in about 20 seconds (chapter 4) or in the back office (chapter 5) — the same rules apply, and staff must ask the scripted consent question.

        The key terms every member accepts
        • We sell to members only, and only to adults (18+). Show your ID whenever we ask.
        • What you buy is for your own use. Don't resell it, pass it on or buy for someone else — that can be a crime, and it ends your membership.
        • Licensed cannabis medicines need your doctor's SAHPRA authorisation, within its limits. Membership alone never entitles you to them.
        • Never drive or operate machinery while impaired.
        • Points and member savings have no cash value and never apply to licensed medicines.
        • We confirm your age with a secure fingerprint of your ID number and keep the records the law requires.
        • Most online orders can be cancelled within 7 days of delivery, and faulty goods can be repaired, replaced or refunded within 6 months.

        Lab results and batch traceability

        Every pack carries a batch code and a QR code that opens /batch/<code>: the public certificate of analysis for that exact lot. The page states plainly when a batch is recalled ("please don't use it"), on hold (quarantine) or past its best-before date, and says "No laboratory testing required" for accessories, apparel and grow gear. Behind it, stock is kept per store × product × batch, sold first-expiry-first-out, and every unit's movement is on an immutable ledger — so a recall can be traced to the stores holding it and the members who bought it in minutes (chapters 5 and 6).

        Rules (CPA s63)
        • A voucher is valid for three years from the day it is issued — not bought (Consumer Protection Act s63(2)); the balance stays on it until then (s63(3)); it can't be exchanged for cash; the daily job marks expired ones.
        • No bonus value, no fees, no discount — and because it is consideration already paid it may lawfully pay for a medicine too (Medicines Act s18A is about inducements). Vouchers are redeemed online (in the bag or at checkout) and at the till, where the voucher is checked on the server and redeemed as the sale posts; refunds return what the voucher paid to the voucher, never as cash.
        • No VAT at issue; VAT is accounted for on the sale the voucher pays for, when it is handed over. HQ's voucher liability is the sum of the active balances.
        • Purchase, issue, redemption and release are all in the audit log.

          • A member is attached by typing the member number from their card ("Taken from their card offline — checked when the till reconnects"). An unknown number is recorded as a compliance exception for HQ to review, not lost.
          • Receipt numbers come from the till's own sequence, so they stay unique; each sale gets a unique id, so a sale is never posted twice however often the sync retries.
          • Queued sales sync every 15 seconds once online (idempotently); the badge shows how many are waiting, and a toast confirms "2 offline sales synced". Anything the server couldn't check offline — an age rule, a limit — is written to the audit log rather than rejecting a sale that already happened.
          Never clear browser storage on a till with queued sales

          The outbox lives in the browser. Check the queue badge is zero before closing the shift or unpairing. If a till must be replaced, sync it first.

            Inventory: stock on hand, stock takes, adjustments, batches and recalls

            Stock on hand

            Every ranged item at your store: on hand, reserved for online orders, available, reorder point and par, sold in 28 days, value at cost, and a status (out of stock, at or below reorder point, overstock). Filters by category and stock status; search by product, SKU or barcode; export CSV. The tiles show stock value at cost, out of stock, at or below reorder point, tied up in overstock, and reserved for online orders.

            Stock takes (full counts and cycle counts)

            1. New stock take: a full count, or a cycle count of one category. Counts are blind by default — counters don't see the system quantity. Only one count can be in progress at a time.

            Buying: purchasing from the DC, network trading, and your prices

            Purchasing (wholesale orders from your distribution centre)

            1. The suggestion comes from a 14-day sales forecast: reorder once what you have and have on order can't cover the 3-day lead time plus safety stock for the swings in daily demand (or falls to the reorder point), and top up to the lead time plus 14 days of cover — never below par, in whole packs, allowing for stock already on order. Lines for items you already hold more than twice par of are flagged. Save a draft or Submit.

A seller accepts (which commits the stock) and ships FEFO; declining puts the units back on the listing; the buyer receives as for a DC order.                 Who trade customers are, and what they can buy

                HQ: the ledger — payments, credit notes, adjustments, statements and holds

                Each account has a ledger: invoices raise what it owes (an order that shipped or was collected; a till sale on account); payments and credit notes lower it; adjustments go either way, always with a note. The balance is simply the sum of the entries. Ageing is FIFO on due dates: a credit that names an order or a sale settles that invoice first (an EFT for its order, a credit note for its sale, a payment HQ recorded against an order); whatever is left, and every credit without a reference, is applied to the oldest due invoice first. The age analysis then buckets what is still open by how far past due it is — current, 1–30, 31–60 and more than 60 days ("90+") — and an unapplied credit shows as negative current, so the total is always the balance.

                The Ledger & statements tab has three forms and two tables:

                • Record a payment — an EFT that showed on the bank statement: Amount received (R), the Bank reference, Received on (blank: today), Settles order (optional: applied to that invoice first, else oldest first) and a note; Record payment. If the account was on hold for an overdue invoice and nothing is past due any more, the hold lifts and the account is emailed ("Hold lifted").
                • Credit note — a return HQ agreed, or a price correction: the amount, an optional reference (else the next TC-000123) and why; Issue credit note. A refund of a trade sale at a till raises its own credit note, numbered as the refund (next section).
                • Adjustment — an HQ correction, signed ("−150.00 reduces what the account owes"), with a note; Post adjustment.
                • Statement · September 2026 — pick a month; opening and closing balances, what was overdue at month end, the month's lines with a running balance; Email statement sends it again by hand.
                • Ledger — every entry newest first: when, kind, the reference (the invoice number, the bank reference, the credit note number) with the order it belongs to and a link to the tax invoice, the due date, debit, credit and the balance after it.

                        Backups and recovery

                        Postgres WAL archiving (point-in-time recovery of at least 7 days) plus a nightly encrypted dump copied off the server to R2 and kept 35 days; slips on disk are copied with it. Quarterly restore drill into staging. Targets: RPO 5 minutes, RTO 1 hour. The stock and loyalty ledgers are append-only, so balances can be rebuilt from movements; the reconciliation queries in the runbook check that stock levels equal the ledger, rollups equal sales, loyalty balances equal the ledger, and that no member saving was ever given on a medicine or to a walk-in.

                        Incidents

                        Till offline
                        Tills keep selling cash (and Yoco); tap, terminal, PayShap, points, licensed items, drops and shifts wait for the connection. Don't clear browser storage; check the queue badge before closing the shift.
                        Card acquiring down
                        Use the bank terminal tender, cash or PayShap; record references.
                        Online payments down
                        Members see "We couldn't open Peach Payments' payment page" and unpaid orders are released after the hold as usual. Peach retries webhooks we didn't answer for 24 hours (Yoco for about a day, then switches the webhook off after 5 days — register it again). A payment that lands after its order was released is logged as payment.review.
                        Product recall
                        HQ › Batches › Recall: unsellable everywhere immediately; stores acknowledge, quarantine and write off (recall return or witnessed destruction); affected members listed and notified; keep the recall report for SAHPRA.
                        Wrong price or promotion live
                        HQ › Pricing / Promotions › end or pause it; tills pick it up on the next refresh; refund affected sales at the till.
                        Suspected data breach
                        Contain (delete sessions, rotate SESSION_SECRET and provider keys), assess scope from the audit log, notify the Information Regulator and affected people as soon as reasonably possible, document everything.

                          Glossary

                          Adult-use mode
                          The HQ switch for a future commercial regime: licensed products to verified adults within a daily limit. Off by default (medical mode).
                          Authorised dispenser
                          A staff member (usually the responsible pharmacist) who may complete sales of licensed medicines; ticked by the owner in Store › Team.
                          Batch / lot
                          A production lot with its own code, expiry and certificate of analysis; stock is kept and sold per batch.
                          Budtender
                          The role name for a cashier or member advisor at the till.
                          Case pack
                          The number of units a trade line is sold in (six oils, twelve coffees, twenty-four papers; one for grow equipment and apparel). Trade orders are placed in whole cases; the till sells any quantity.
                          Cash-up
                          A closed till shift: float, cash taken, counted, variance — the basis for banking.
                          COA
                          Certificate of analysis: the laboratory's results for a batch, public at /batch/<code> and as a panel on the product page.
                          Collection code
                          The 6-digit code (and QR) a member shows to collect an online order; verified at handover with an ID check.
                          Credit note
                          A negative entry on a trade account's ledger: a return or price correction HQ agreed (numbered TC-000001…), or the on-account part of a refunded trade sale (numbered as the refund). It settles the invoice it names first.
                          Customer display
                          A second screen (/pos/display) that mirrors the till's sale for the member being served: lines, deals, points, the total, the thank-you.
                          DC
                          Distribution centre: a warehouse store that supplies retail stores and ships courier orders.
                          Drop
                          A limited, members-only release with tier early access and a per-member limit. Never medicines.
                          FEFO / FIFO
                          First expiry, first out (how stock is picked); first in, first out (how cost is taken).
                          Founding Member
                          One of the first members on the launch list, with a numbered card and a tier floor for a set period.
                          Gift voucher
                          Prepaid value (R200, R500 or R1,000) with a code, valid three years, redeemed online in the bag or at checkout; money, never a promotion.
                          Handling fee
                          What a trade account pays (R75 ex VAT by default) for a click & collect order a store packs and hands over; it is part of the sale posted at that store.
                          Incentive-excluded
                          Classes that get no promotion, member price, markdown, points, deal, gift, drop or commission (Medicines Act s18A): licensed products in medical mode and the "no incentives" classes.
                          Instant saving / member price
                          The tier percentage (or a member price list) applied the moment a member is identified, at the till and online.
                          Members only
                          The rule that every sale needs a registered member.
                          My Deals
                          A weekly personal offer on a member's favourite eligible product, HQ-funded and credited to the store.
                          Network trading
                          Stores buying and selling surplus stock with each other, cleared by HQ on the statement.
                          On account
                          A trade account's credit terms: a sale or order is invoiced on HQ's trade ledger and falls due after the account's payment days, within its credit limit. Never money at the time of sale.
                          Opening checklist
                          The tasks HQ ticks off before a store opens — legal, set-up, people, launch — seeded when the store is onboarded, with due dates counted back from the opening day.
                          Par / reorder point
                          Order up to par when stock is at or below the reorder point; both set per item per store.
                          Patient authorisation
                          A SAHPRA Section 21 authorisation registered against a member, with a monthly quantity.
                          Reorder reminder
                          A message a member asked for, every 14–90 days, with a one-tap link that puts a consumable in their bag; nothing is charged.
                          Rollups
                          Daily per-store and per-variant totals updated with every sale, so network reports never scan raw sales.
                          RRP
                          Recommended retail price set by HQ, VAT included; franchisees set their own shelf price.
                          Shelf price
                          A store's own price for its tills.
                          Statement
                          The monthly franchise statement between HQ and a store, netted to one amount.
                          Tier
                          Seed, Sprout, Bloom, Harvest — by rolling 12-month spend, each with an instant saving.
                          Today list
                          The store's to-do list at the top of the overview — orders, alerts, cash, updates, expiring lots, low stock, counts, authorisations, invoices — most urgent first.
                          Trade account
                          A business approved to buy at trade prices (TA-000001…): its legal details for the tax invoice, the ranges it may buy, prepaid or on-account terms, a credit limit and payment days, its buyers and its ledger.
                          Trade order
                          An order placed in the trade portal (TO-000001…): whole cases at ex-VAT prices, by courier from the DC or click & collect at a store, paid by EFT against a pro forma or on account; invoiced TI-000001… when it ships or is collected.
                          Training mode
                          The till on a sandbox: practice sales numbered TRAINING 0001…, nothing posted, printed for real, moved or awarded; started and ended with a manager's PIN.
                          Z-report / X-report
                          The shift's closing report / the live report during the shift.
