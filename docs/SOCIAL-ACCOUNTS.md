# Social accounts: @herbalholland on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok

The owner's guide to opening the three accounts, dressing them with the kit, connecting them to Postiz and posting the
pinned set. Everything you upload is in `public/brand/social` (made by `npx tsx scripts/social-kit.tsx`), and the one
link every bio carries is the link-in-bio page, **shop.herbalholland.com/go**.

Social is brand-led only. Meta and TikTok ban the promotion of cannabis and CBD products, whatever the licence, so the
accounts talk about the brand, the neighbourhood, the stores and Herbal Holland Rewards, and never about products,
prices, consumption or what anything does for you. The only call to action is joining Rewards (the Founding Members
list) at shop.herbalholland.com/club/join. Section 5 has the rules in full.

## 1. Before you start

- One email for all three accounts: **admin@herbalholland.com** (a shared mailbox HQ controls, not a person's).
- A second person as admin on every account from day one (the marketing lead, or a director). If one phone is lost
  or one person leaves, the accounts stay with the company.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) on everything, with an authenticator app (not SMS), and the backup codes in the
  company password manager.
- The handle is **@herbalholland** everywhere. If a platform says it's taken, the fallback is **@herbalholland.za** (the
  same on all three; don't mix). Check all three before creating any, so you don't end up with one of each.
- The brand name as the display name: **Herbal Holland**. Not "Herbal Holland Dispensary", "Herbal Holland CBD" or
  anything with a product word in it.

## 2. Meta: Business Portfolio → Facebook Page → Instagram

Meta's accounts hang off a **Business Portfolio** (Meta Business Suite). Do it in this order, so the Page and the
Instagram account are owned by the company and not by whoever's personal profile clicked first.

1. **Business Portfolio.** business.facebook.com → Create a business portfolio: name *Herbal Holland*, your name, and
   admin@herbalholland.com. Under Settings → Users, add the second admin (full control). Turn 2FA on for the
   portfolio (Settings → Security Centre → require 2FA for everyone).
2. **Facebook Page.** In the portfolio: Accounts → Pages → Add → Create a new Page. Name *Herbal Holland*. Category:
   **Retail company** (and, as a second category, *Shopping & retail*). Not "Cannabis store", "Alternative & holistic
   health", "Medical supply" or "Vape shop": those categories go straight to review and the Page is restricted or
   removed. Username (the handle): herbalholland. Profile picture `avatar-ink.png`, cover `facebook-cover.png`.
   About: the bio below. Add the website (shop.herbalholland.com/go), the store addresses once the leases are signed,
   and the contact email. Page settings → Audience and visibility → set a **minimum age of 18** for the Page.
3. **Instagram.** Create the Instagram account from the app with admin@herbalholland.com (a fresh account, not
   converted from someone's personal one): username herbalholland, name Herbal Holland. Settings → Account type →
   switch to a **Business** account, category *Retail company* (hide the category label if you like). Then link it:
   Settings → Business tools and controls → Connect a Facebook Page → the Page above. Back in the Business Portfolio
   (Accounts → Instagram accounts) confirm it shows under the portfolio. Settings → Privacy → set the **minimum age to
   18** (Instagram → Settings → Audience: 18+).
4. Profile picture `avatar-ink.png`. Bio below. Website: https://shop.herbalholland.com/go. Add the five highlights
   as empty highlights first (Rewards, Stores, Lab-tested, Founding, Learn), with the highlight covers, then fill
   them from the stories you post.

## 3. TikTok

1. Download TikTok and sign up with the email admin@herbalholland.com (not with a Facebook or Google login). Username
   herbalholland, name Herbal Holland.
2. Settings → Account → switch to a **Business account**. Category: **Retail & shopping** (not Health, Pharmacy,
   Beauty or Alternative medicine).
3. Settings → Security → 2-step verification (authenticator app). Add the second admin's phone as a backup, and
   write the recovery codes down with the others.
4. Profile picture `avatar-ink.png`. Bio below. Website link: https://shop.herbalholland.com/go (Business accounts
   get the link straight away). Content preference: set the account's audience to **adults 18+** when posting (each
   video's Audience controls).

   **posted by hand** from the Studio (copy the words, save the card) and not connected to Postiz.

What **not** to put in a bio, username, name or category (each one has got brands reviewed and removed):

- cannabis, marijuana, weed, dagga, THC, CBD, hemp, 420, strain names, "dispensary", "vape", "edibles", "pre-rolls";
- the leaf, smoke or cloud emoji, "high", "stoner", "medicinal", "relief", "healing" and any other benefit claim;

  which links on from there);
- hashtags in the bio. The marketing plan has the hashtag rules for posts: none that name the plant.

## 5. The platform rules, and how we stay inside them

What gets a cannabis brand banned: Meta's and TikTok's policies treat cannabis and CBD as restricted goods, and a
business account exists to sell, so the systems look for *the sale* rather than the plant. A bio or name with a
product word in it, a category like "Cannabis store", a post that shows a product, a price, a "shop now" or a link to a
product page, a video of anyone consuming anything, a claim that a product calms, helps you sleep, treats or relieves
anything (a medical claim is the fastest route to removal, and also illegal for us under the Medicines Act), a giveaway
of product, a paid boost of any post (Meta rejects cannabis and CBD ads and flags the account that tried), buying
followers, or being reported by a competitor while any of the above is on the profile. First the reach is quietly
cut, then posts are removed, then the account is restricted and finally removed, usually with no person to appeal to.

How we stay inside the lines: the accounts are the brand, the two towns and the people, never the product. Posts show
the stores being built, the teams, the neighbourhood, the mark, the merch (apparel and accessories are fine; nothing
that holds or burns anything), the Learn articles as plain-English explainers, and one ask: join Herbal Holland
Rewards, free, at the link in the bio. Every post carries "Adults 18+" and the account's audience is set to 18+. No
prices, no product photos, no consumption, no claims, no leaf, no smoke, no 4/20, no hashtags that name the plant, no
boosting, no giveaways of product, no replying to comments with product advice (send them to the website, a store or
WhatsApp). The Studio runs every caption through the same rules (`screenCopy`) before it's approved, and anything
that fails is reworded, not posted. If a platform does restrict the account, don't argue in the appeal: fix the thing
it named, appeal once with "corrected", and carry on.

## 6. Connect to Postiz

Postiz (docs/INTEGRATIONS.md has the install) publishes the Studio's posts to Facebook and Instagram at their time.
Connect each channel from the account that owns it, after the accounts above are set up and have their profile
pictures, so the previews in Postiz look right.

1. Sign in to Postiz (social.herbalholland.com) as HQ.
2. **Settings → Add channel → Facebook Page.** Sign in with the Facebook login of the Business Portfolio admin, allow
   the permissions, pick the *Herbal Holland* Page. Postiz lists it with the avatar.
3. **Settings → Add channel → Instagram (Facebook Business).** The same login; pick the Instagram account linked to the
   Page. Choose this one, not *Instagram Standalone*, so the connection is through the Page the portfolio owns.
4. Threads, if used: **Add channel → Threads**, the same Instagram login.
5. **TikTok: don't add it.** The API audit keeps posts private, and TikTok's policy bans our category; it's posted by
   hand. In the Studio, each TikTok post is marked **I'll post it by hand**.
6. In Postiz, open each channel's **Preferences** and set the customer name to *Herbal Holland* and the time zone to
   Africa/Johannesburg. Send one test post from Postiz (the welcome card with the words "Testing, back in a minute"),
   check it appears on both, delete it from both.
7. Settings → Public API → copy the key into the platform's `POSTIZ_API_KEY` (and `POSTIZ_URL`) if that isn't done
   yet, and confirm HQ → Marketing → Social shows *Connected*.

The Meta app tokens Postiz uses expire if the admin's password changes or their 2FA is reset: when a post fails with
"token", reconnect the channel in Postiz (Settings → the channel → Reconnect) and release the post again from the Studio.
